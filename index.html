How to use Guitarify

  1. Open the Detect tab and tap the circle to start recording.
  2. Play each note of your chord one at a time, and let each note ring for 1 to 2 seconds.
  3. Tap the circle again to stop. Guitarify shows the chord and where to play it on the fretboard.

Troubleshooting

  • "Detection failed": Guitarify needs an internet connection to analyze audio. Check your connection and try again.
  • No chord detected: Play in a quiet room, one note at a time, and let each note ring.
  • Microphone permission: If you tapped "Don't Allow", turn it on under Settings > Guitarify > Microphone.
