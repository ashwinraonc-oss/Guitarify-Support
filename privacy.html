Guitarify Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Guitarify identifies the chord you play and shows it on a guitar fretboard. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What we collect

Guitarify does not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or any other personal information.

When you use chord detection, the app records a short audio clip from your microphone, only while you have started a recording. The clip is sent securely (HTTPS) to our server, which analyzes it to find the notes and chord and sends the result back to the app. The audio is deleted right after it is analyzed and is not stored, shared, sold, or used for advertising or tracking.

The tuner uses the microphone only on your device and does not send audio anywhere.

Our server

Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs, such as the time of a request and the network address it came from, as part of operating the service. These logs do not contain your audio.

Tracking and third parties

Guitarify does not track you, does not contain advertising, and does not share data with third parties for marketing.

Microphone permission

You can turn off microphone access at any time in Settings > Guitarify. Chord detection and tuning will not work without it.

Children

Guitarify is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
